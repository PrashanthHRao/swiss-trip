  • 🧡 Swiss Travel Pass — 6 days, 2nd class, ≈ CHF 399 (Sat 1 – Thu 6 Aug). One pass covers the whole table — no per-journey tickets.
  • 🎉 Free with the pass: Rigi, all lake boats, city transport and 500+ museums. Not covered: paragliding and the First adventure rides (First Flyer, Mountain Cart, Trotti) — book those separately.
  • Book ahead online: Jungfraujoch (seat reservation +CHF 10, May–Oct) and Lindt museum (CHF 17, time slot).
  • Use the SBB app for live times & platforms.

💡 Tips

  • Days 3 & 4 are interchangeable — check the MeteoSwiss forecast the night before. If the summit is clear, do Jungfraujoch; if the valley is sunny, fly and visit First/Lauterbrunnen.
  • Book paragliding AND Jungfraujoch in advance — early August is peak season
  • Sunny, layers, sunglasses — it's alpine winter on the Jungfraujoch even in August (t-shirt down below, jacket up top)
  • Carry passports — needed at both airports
  • Mountain restaurants take cards, but keep ~CHF 100 cash for stalls and lockers
  • Golden Hour on Harder Kulm is sunrise — worth one early morning
